About JAMES

Your local, private AI assistant running entirely in the browser.

What is JAMES?

JAMES is a next-generation, browser-native AI assistant designed with absolute privacy at its core. Unlike traditional AI services that route your data through remote cloud servers, JAMES executes completely on your local device using WebGPU, WebAssembly, and ONNX Runtime Web.

Every session starts completely fresh, ensuring no chat history or personal data ever leaves your browser.

Key Features

  • 100% Client-Side Inference: Powered locally in your browser with zero server tracking or telemetry.
  • Real-Time Tool Suite: Equipped with built-in tools for web search, weather, currency conversion, Wikipedia summaries, timezones, and more.
  • Multiple Operation Modes: Optimized for standard desktop use, lightweight fallback devices, and TV/remote control displays.
  • Lightning Fast Small-Talk: Instant response handling for greetings and common queries without waiting for model inference.
